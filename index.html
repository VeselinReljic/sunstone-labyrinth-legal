Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sunstone Labyrinth (the "App") collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. The App is developed and published by Veselin Reljić ("we", "us", "the Developer").

In short: Sunstone Labyrinth does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, or contact list, and stores your game progress only on your own device. The only data that reaches anyone else comes from Apple's or Google's own ad and billing systems when you view an ad or make a purchase, and that data is handled under their privacy policies, not ours.

1Information we collect

Game data, stored locally only. Your level progress, settings (sound, music, haptics, control scheme, language, character color), and power-up inventory are saved with your device's local app storage. This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us.

No account required. The App has no sign-up, login, or user profile of any kind. We never collect your name, email address, or contacts.

Purchases and subscriptions. The App offers a monthly subscription and a one-time "lifetime" unlock. These are processed entirely by Apple's or Google's own in-app billing — possibly through a purchase-management layer such as RevenueCat — which receives your purchase receipt and subscription status so the App can grant your entitlement. We never see or store your card or payment details ourselves.

  • Apple App Store / Apple Pay purchases: governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play purchases: governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • A purchase-management layer (if used, e.g. RevenueCat): governed by that provider's own privacy policy, disclosed here once integrated.

Advertising (planned). The App does not yet serve real ads. Once an ad network (such as Google AdMob or Meta Audience Network) is integrated, it may collect a device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), IP address, and ad interaction data, to show ads and measure their performance — including personalized ads where you've given consent. EU/UK/Swiss users will see a consent prompt (via a certified consent management platform) before any personalized-ads data is collected, and can withdraw consent at any time from that same prompt or the App's settings. On iOS, the App additionally requests App Tracking Transparency permission before any cross-app tracking identifier is used for ads, as required by Apple — declining it is always an option and the App continues to work normally. This section will be updated, with a new "last updated" date above, once a real ad network goes live, naming the specific network(s) used.

Crash reporting and analytics (planned). We intend to add Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics to help us find and fix bugs and understand how the App is used — for example, which levels are played, completed, or restarted, and diagnostic data from a crash (device model, OS version, and the app state around the crash). Neither is integrated yet; this section will be updated, with a new "last updated" date above, once either goes live. Both are governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and this data is not linked to your name or contact details, since the App collects neither.

2How information is used

Locally stored game data is used only to run the game and remember your progress between sessions. Any data processed by an ad network or app store is used to display ads, process payments, and prevent fraud, per that provider's own policy.

3Children's privacy

Sunstone Labyrinth is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we will address it.

4Data retention and your choices

Because your game data lives only on your device, you control it directly: clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App removes it. There is no server-side account for us to delete, since none exists. To reset your in-app progress without uninstalling, use the reset option in the App's Settings menu.

5Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what data is processed about you and to request its deletion. Since we hold no personal data on our own servers, most such requests are best directed to Apple or Google for data tied to your purchases or advertising identifier — we're glad to help point you the right way if you contact us.

6Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to an app that stores data locally on-device. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and this policy does not guarantee absolute security.

7Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves — for example, when a new ad network or analytics provider is integrated. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to solvedgames@duck.com.
